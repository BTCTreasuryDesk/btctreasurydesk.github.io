The 5-minute guide to Bitcoin treasury companies

Five short answers, with links to the glossary and episodes.

01 | Guide

Five questions

  1. 01

    What is a Bitcoin treasury company?

    A public company that holds bitcoin as a main reserve asset and raises money to buy more. Strategy (MSTR) is the largest, with 848,250 BTC as of Oct 11, 2026. (Strategy 8-K, SEC EDGAR). Strive (ASST), Metaplanet, Twenty One and others run their own versions. Owning the stock is not owning bitcoin: shareholders own a slice of a company that also has debt, preferred stock and expenses. See treasury company and the leaderboard.

  2. 02

    How does Strategy pay for its bitcoin?

    Mostly by selling securities: new common shares through at-the-market (ATM) offerings, convertible notes, and preferred stock such as STRC, STRF, STRK and STRD, which Strategy calls digital credit. Each week it buys, Strategy files an 8-K listing what it sold, what it raised and how much bitcoin it bought (EDGAR). How it works on the show: Ep. 11.

  3. 03

    What is mNAV?

    mNAV compares the company’s market value with the value of its bitcoin. Above 1x, the market pays a premium; selling new shares can then add bitcoin per share (BTC per share). Strategy defines its mNAV as enterprise value (market cap plus debt and preferred stock, minus cash) divided by the market value of its bitcoin (8-K Ex. 99.2, Dec 1, 2025), so it reads higher than a simple market-cap version. Below 1x, issuing shares would reduce bitcoin per share. The live figure is on the desk and Strategy page. Explained in Ep. 11.

  4. 04

    What are the preferreds?

    Shares that pay a stated dividend on a $100 stated amount. They rank ahead of common stock but behind debt; among Strategy’s, STRF is most senior, then STRC, STRE, STRK and STRD (10-Q, Q2 2026). STRF and STRD pay fixed rates (10%); STRK pays 8% and can convert into MSTR. STRC’s rate is variable and reset by Strategy, 12.00% for periods from Oct 16, 2026 (8-K, Oct 1, 2026); Strive’s SATA is also variable, 13.00% for October 2026 (8-K, Sep 15, 2026). STRD is non-cumulative: a skipped dividend is never owed. Compare them on the Preferred Yield Desk.

  5. 05

    What are the risks?

    Bitcoin is volatile. If the premium (mNAV) shrinks, issuing shares stops adding bitcoin per share. Preferred dividends and debt interest must be paid, and dividends can change or be suspended. Strategy keeps a USD Reserve for that, and between Jun 30 and Aug 10, 2026 its ledger showed net BTC reductions. Index rules matter too: in Oct 2026 MSCI adopted a rule that could remove Strategy and Metaplanet from its indexes (MSCI, Oct 15, 2026). This is education, not advice: read the filings and do your own research.
