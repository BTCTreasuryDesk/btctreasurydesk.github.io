Formulas

market cap = share price × diluted shares
BTC NAV = BTC held × BTC price
mNAV = market cap ÷ BTC NAV · premium = mNAV − 1
implied BTC price = market cap ÷ BTC held (the BTC price at which mNAV would be 1)
BTC NAV per share = BTC held ÷ diluted shares × BTC price

This is a simple equity-only mNAV. Strategy’s own mNAV (1.20x on strategy.com, 10/02/2026 04:00 PM ET) uses enterprise value (adds debt and preferred stock, subtracts cash), so it reads higher than this simple version.

03 | Months to cover premium

How long BTC Yield takes to cover the premium

…

Reference points, computed by us: Strategy BTC per share -6.2% from Sep 22, 2025 to Sep 28, 2026 (ledger holdings ÷ ADSO); Strive +18.1% from Jul 24, 2026 to Sep 25, 2026 (not annualized). Past changes don’t predict future ones.

Formula

months to cover premium = (mNAV − 1) ÷ (BTC Yield ÷ 12)
A simple, non-compounding rule of thumb: how many months of BTC-per-share growth it would take to “earn” today’s premium. If the yield is zero or negative, or mNAV ≤ 1, the result isn’t meaningful.
