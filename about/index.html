About the show

BTC Treasury Desk is a YouTube show with news and analysis on public companies that hold bitcoin as a treasury asset. Coverage centers on Strategy (MSTR and its STRC preferred) and Strive (ASST and its SATA preferred), with other treasury companies when the news calls for it.

Episodes walk through bitcoin purchases, SEC filings, capital raises, preferred dividends, mNAV and price trends, in plain English and with sources on screen.

Format

  • Desk episodes: the latest filings and moves, in a few minutes.
  • Deep Dives: one topic in more depth.
  • Weekend Desk: the week in review.

Host

The show is created and hosted by Phil, a Navy veteran with about nine years in bank sanctions compliance. That background shapes the approach: primary sources first, numbers checked, no hype.

Episodes are presented on air by the desk’s anchor voice, Clara West.
