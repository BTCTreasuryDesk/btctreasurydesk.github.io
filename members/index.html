    Model read. Education only, not investment advice.

    The data behind it
    CompanymNAVBTC/share growth

    mNAV = market value vs the bitcoin it holds; methods differ by source. BTC per share growth only where filings give both BTC held and share count.

    BTC Yield tracker sats per share, from filings

    Model read. Education only, not investment advice.

    BTC Yield

    BTC Yield = % change in BTC per (assumed diluted) share over the period. It is a company KPI, not a return on the stock.

    Cycle Clock

    Model read. Education only, not investment advice.

    Halving cycle

    Next halving at block 1,050,000. Date estimated from the current block height at 10 minutes per block, so it drifts by days. Past cycles are only four data points.
