01 | Privacy

Privacy note

Last updated October 12, 2026. btctreasurydesk.com is a static website. It has no user accounts and sets no advertising cookies. See also the Terms of Use.

Email (Kit)

If you subscribe, your email address and the choices you tick (weekly Desk email, new-material alerts, the downloads you pick) are sent to our email provider, Kit (ConvertKit), and used only to send those emails. Each email has an unsubscribe link. We don’t sell your address.

Data kept on your device

Your settings stay in your browser’s local storage: home layout, tiles and price scroll, chart choices, the “My stack” tracker, Members tools (saved plans and setups), the Members unlock, Desk Mode layout and music choice. They never leave your device unless you use Export settings yourself. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Analytics

If we use analytics, it is cookieless and privacy-friendly (such as GoatCounter): aggregate page counts and referrers, no personal profiles, no cross-site tracking, no selling of data.

Third-party content

Pages load live data and widgets from third parties, which receive your IP address and browser details like any website you visit: Coinbase and CoinGecko (crypto prices, dominance), CNBC quotes (stock prices), mempool.space (network stats), alternative.me (Fear & Greed), TradingView (charts and some quotes), X (embedded posts) and YouTube (only after you press play; the player uses youtube-nocookie.com). Their own privacy policies apply.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.
