Profile

Michael J. Saylor founded the company now called Strategy in November 1989 and served as its Chairman and CEO until August 2022. [Strategy 2026 proxy statement]

Since August 2022 he has been Chairman of the Board and Executive Chairman. Before founding the company, he worked at DuPont as a venture manager (1988–89) and at Federal Group as a consultant (1987–88). [Strategy 2026 proxy statement]

In August 2020, as CEO, he led the decision to make bitcoin the company’s primary treasury reserve asset, starting with a $250 million purchase. [MicroStrategy press release, Aug 11, 2020]

Under his leadership, Strategy funds bitcoin purchases by issuing common stock, convertible notes and preferred stock, which the company calls “digital credit.” [Strategy 10-Q for Q2 2026]
