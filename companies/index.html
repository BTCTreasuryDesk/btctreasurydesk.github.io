  • ▲ Fast-growing BTC holdings up 25% or more over the last 90 days.
  • ▼ Reducing BTC holdings down 10% or more over the last 90 days (sold or otherwise reduced).
  • ▲/▼ Price share price moved ±30% or more over 30 days, measured from our own daily data snapshots (appears once a 30-day snapshot history exists).

The 90-day change compares today’s holdings with the last dated balance on or before the window start (Strategy: its own ledger; others: dated holdings updates on BitcoinTreasuries.NET).
